About

I hold a B.Sc. in Mathematics from Obafemi Awolowo University, Nigeria, and an M.Sc. in Mathematical Engineering from the University of L'Aquila, Italy, with coursework spanning PDEs, functional analysis, numerical analysis, optimisation, and dynamical systems. My Master's thesis (details) derived and numerically investigated mixing-rate bounds for a passive scalar advected by incompressible, enstrophy-constrained flow.

That verify-first approach now runs through a portfolio of verified numerical solvers and models: a pseudo-spectral Navier–Stokes solver with a POD-Galerkin reduced-order model, a FEniCSx finite-element CFD solver benchmarked against published references, a PDE-constrained Bayesian inverse problem, a phase-field multiphase-flow solver, SIMP topology optimisation, and an Ensemble Kalman Filter for chaotic data assimilation. In parallel, I have built and deployed machine learning systems in industry, including aviation telemetry classifiers and FFT-based signal-processing pipelines, translating mathematical and computational ideas into working software.

Current Research Interests

I am interested in combining PDE-based mathematical models, numerical simulation, and machine learning to develop surrogate, reduced-order, and inverse models for physical systems, including fluid dynamics and data assimilation. A recurring theme in my work is understanding where data-driven approximations agree with the underlying physics, where they fail, and how those discrepancies can be quantified: a snapshot-POD model that fails on unseen flow realisations, or a Bayesian posterior whose effective sample size is too low to trust, are as informative as a model that succeeds. I am particularly interested in extending this work toward probabilistic and uncertainty-aware methods, including Bayesian and ensemble-based uncertainty quantification for learned PDE surrogates and data-efficient learning in settings where labelled data are expensive, sparse, or physically constrained.
