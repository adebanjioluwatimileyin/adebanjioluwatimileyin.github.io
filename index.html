About

My path into research began with a BSc in Mathematics from Obafemi Awolowo University, Nigeria, followed by an MSc in Mathematical Engineering at the University of L'Aquila, Italy. My thesis, Lower Bounds on the Mix Norm of Passive Scalars Advected by Incompressible Enstrophy-Constrained Flows, combined variational analysis and functional inequalities with pseudo-spectral numerical simulation to study optimal mixing in incompressible flows.

That grounding in mathematical analysis and numerical methods now shapes how I approach machine learning. I am interested in problems where a model has to respect an underlying physical or mathematical structure — inverse problems, computational imaging, and PDE-constrained systems — rather than treating learning as a black box.

Alongside this research direction, I have built production ML systems in industry: aviation telemetry classifiers and signal-processing pipelines at Intuos, predictive failure models at N-and Italia, and geometric algorithms at Bridgestone. I have also developed end-to-end LLM systems, including a medical RAG assistant and LoRA/QLoRA fine-tuned models.

I am currently seeking PhD and research-engineering opportunities in scientific machine learning, inverse problems, computational imaging, PDEs, and optimization.

Research · Scientific ML · PDEs

Optimal Mixing of Passive Scalars

Code ↗
Problem
How efficiently can an incompressible, enstrophy-constrained velocity field mix a passive scalar, and what are the theoretical limits on that mixing rate?
Method
Derived analytical lower bounds on the H⁻¹ mix norm using variational and functional-analytic methods, then simulated the Lin–Thiffeault–Doering optimal mixing velocity on the 2-torus with a pseudo-spectral solver, Leray projection, and RK45 time integration.
Result
Confirmed exponential H⁻¹ decay across initial conditions, with mixing timescale scaling as predicted by the theoretical lower bound as the support parameter a varies.

3D Liver Segmentation — Abdominal CT

Code ↗
Problem
Automatic segmentation of liver structures from abdominal CT volumes for downstream clinical and computational analysis.
Method
3D U-Net trained end-to-end on 131 MSD Task03 liver CT cases, with foreground-biased patch sampling, a combined soft-Dice + BCE loss, cosine learning-rate annealing, and Gaussian sliding-window inference.
Result
Best validation Dice 0.9886, HD95 ≤ 1 mm, after 200 epochs on a single Kaggle T4 GPU.

03
Research · Inverse Rendering

Differentiable PBR — Inverse Material Optimisation

Code ↗
Problem
Recovering spatially-varying surface material properties — albedo, normal, roughness, metallic — from only a set of multi-view photographs.
Method
Pure-PyTorch differentiable renderer implementing the Cook-Torrance BRDF (GGX normal distribution, Schlick Fresnel, Smith geometry term), optimizing material maps directly via gradient descent against 8 reference views — no external differentiable-rasterization library.
Result
Converged material maps after 2,000 optimization steps, reaching an MSE of ~1×10⁻⁶ against target renders.
PyTorchCook-Torrance BRDFDifferentiable Rendering
04
Research · Neural Rendering

NeRF vs TensoRF — Neural Radiance Fields

Code ↗
Problem
Reconstructing a continuous 3D scene representation from posed 2D images, and understanding the trade-off between an implicit MLP representation and an explicit tensor-decomposed one.
Method
Implemented vanilla NeRF and TensoRF (vector-matrix decomposition, Chen et al. 2022) from scratch in PyTorch, including an MPS-safe gather-based interpolation scheme for Apple Silicon, and compared them under matched conditions.
Result
TensoRF reached 17.2 dB PSNR (+3 dB over vanilla NeRF's 14.2 dB) in half the training iterations (15k vs 30k).
PyTorchNeRFTensoRFVolume Rendering

Aircraft Engine Monitoring via Audio DSP

Code ↗
Problem
Estimating aircraft engine RPM and operational status in real time without dedicated tachometer hardware.

Result
Validated against a real Tecnam P92 / Rotax 912 engine, demonstrating accurate RPM extraction from acoustic signal alone.
