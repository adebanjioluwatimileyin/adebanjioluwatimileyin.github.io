Research Results

Optimal Mixing of Passive Scalars — MSc Thesis
Python pseudo-spectral simulation of the Lin–Thiffeault–Doering optimal mixing velocity on the 2-torus. View thesis & code ↗
Scalar field evolution (a = 0.5) Six snapshots of the passive scalar θ advected by the optimal velocity field. The initially localised blob is stretched and filamented by the flow, driving the H⁻¹ mix norm to zero.
Initial conditions (a = 0.5) Four families of initial data — sinusoidal, diagonal, strip, and trigonometric polynomial — all L²-normalised and supported in [0, a]².
H⁻¹ mix norm decay & Lp conservation Left: log ‖θ‖H⁻¹ decays linearly — confirming exponential mixing. Right: L², L⁴, L⁸ norms remain ≈ 1, verifying the scheme conserves scalar statistics.
Multi-scale sweep: mixing timescale vs support parameter a Left: mix norm decay for a ∈ {0.50, 0.625, 0.75, 0.875} — larger support produces slower mixing. Right: mixing timescale (−1/slope) grows with a, confirming the power-law scaling predicted by the lower bound theory.
