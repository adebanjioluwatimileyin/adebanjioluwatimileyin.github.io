About

I hold a B.Sc. in Mathematics from Obafemi Awolowo University, Nigeria, and an M.Sc. in Mathematical Engineering from the University of L'Aquila, Italy, with coursework spanning PDEs, functional analysis, numerical analysis, optimisation, and dynamical systems. My Master's thesis, Lower Bounds on the Mix Norm of Passive Scalars Advected by Incompressible Enstrophy-Constrained Flows, derived analytical bounds on passive-scalar mixing in constrained incompressible flows and validated them against a pseudo-spectral numerical solver.

That foundation now shapes my current work extending phase-field methods for multiphase-flow PDEs toward learning-augmented solvers, alongside research in inverse problems, computational imaging, and physics-informed neural networks. In parallel, I have built and deployed machine learning systems in industry, including aviation telemetry classifiers and FFT-based signal-processing pipelines, translating mathematical and computational ideas into working software.
