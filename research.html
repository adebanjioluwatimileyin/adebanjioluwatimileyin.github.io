Research

Scientific Machine Learning

How can learning-based methods complement, rather than replace, the mathematical structure of physical systems?

Foundation

PDE theory and numerical analysis provide the physical constraints, conservation laws, boundary conditions, and known symmetries, that a learned model should respect.

Approach

Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) enforce the PDE residual, initial condition, and boundary condition directly through automatic differentiation, so the network trains without labelled interior data.

Current research

Extending a phase-field solver for multiphase-flow PDEs, coupling an Allen-Cahn interface model, incompressible Navier-Stokes equations, and an energy equation with an FFT-based pressure solver, from a validated 2D/1D prototype toward 3D, as a foundation for future learning-augmented solvers.

Selected work

  • → PINN: 1D Advection-Diffusion
  • → Phase-Field Multiphase-Flow Modelling

Applied Mathematics & PDEs

What are the fundamental limits on mixing, transport, and diffusion in a constrained PDE system, and can they be derived analytically rather than only observed numerically?

Foundation

Functional analysis, variational methods, and functional inequalities.

Approach

Deriving analytical bounds, for example on the H⁻¹ mix norm of a passive scalar under an enstrophy-constrained velocity field, then validating them computationally with pseudo-spectral and finite-element solvers.

Current research

This bound-then-validate approach, first developed for the mixing problem in my Master's thesis, now underlies the phase-field multiphase-flow work above: state the structure analytically, then build the solver that tests it.

Selected work

  • → M.Sc. Thesis: Optimal Mixing of Passive Scalars
  • → Phase-Field PDE Modelling

Inverse Problems & Optimisation

Given corrupted, partial, or indirect observations, how do you recover the underlying signal, image, or physical parameters?

Foundation

Variational formulations and convex/non-convex optimisation; for differentiable rendering, gradient-based optimisation through a physical forward model.

Approach

Total-variation regularisation solved via Douglas-Rachford splitting for image restoration; a differentiable physically-based renderer (Cook-Torrance BRDF) that recovers material maps by backpropagating through the rendering equation itself.

Current research

The differentiable-rendering work marks a shift from pixel-space restoration (TV regularisation) toward recovering physical parameters directly, by backpropagating through a forward model rather than a fixed prior.

Selected work

  • → Differentiable PBR Material Recovery
  • → TV-Regularised Image Inpainting

Computer Vision & Medical Imaging

How do you extract reliable structure, an organ boundary, a 3D scene, a clean image, from noisy or high-dimensional visual data?

Foundation

Convolutional and volumetric deep learning architectures, combined with domain-specific priors: foreground-biased sampling for class-imbalanced medical volumes, tensor decomposition for efficient 3D scene representation.

Approach

A 3D U-Net for liver segmentation from abdominal CT, trained with a combined Dice/BCE loss and sliding-window inference; from-scratch implementations of NeRF and TensoRF for neural scene reconstruction, compared quantitatively under matched conditions.

Current research

The two most recent projects sit at opposite ends of the same problem: extracting structure from a fixed volumetric grid (CT) versus from unstructured, view-based signals (NeRF/TensoRF).

Selected work

  • → 3D Liver Segmentation from Abdominal CT
  • → NeRF vs TensoRF Neural Radiance Fields

Signal Processing & Engineering Systems

How do you extract a physically meaningful signal from real-world sensor data, and turn it into a reliable operational pipeline?

Foundation

Fourier analysis and spectral estimation.

Approach

FFT-based frequency-peak detection for engine RPM estimation from acoustic signal alone, and telemetry-based classifiers for real-time flight-phase identification.

Current research

Both pipelines are deployed and operational, an FFT-based monitor validated on a real Tecnam P92/Rotax 912 engine, and a flight-phase classifier running in production at Intuos Srl, rather than left as offline analyses.

Selected work

  • → Aircraft Engine RPM Estimation via Audio DSP
  • → Flight Data Monitoring at Intuos Srl

Future Directions

I am particularly interested in extending this work toward probabilistic and uncertainty-aware formulations: quantifying confidence in learned surrogates for PDE-constrained systems, and data-efficient learning where labelled data is expensive or physically constrained, as is typical in scientific and engineering settings.
