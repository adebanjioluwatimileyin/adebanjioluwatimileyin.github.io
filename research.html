Research

Research Interests

  • Scientific Machine Learning — combining mathematical models, numerical methods, and learning-based approaches for scientific and engineering problems, where the structure of the underlying system informs the model.
  • Inverse Problems & Computational Imaging — image restoration, inverse and differentiable rendering, medical image segmentation, reconstruction, optimization.
  • PDEs & Numerical Methods — functional analysis, variational methods, finite element and pseudo-spectral methods, optimization over function spaces, fluid dynamics.
  • Signal Processing & Engineering Systems — FFT-based signal processing, aircraft engine monitoring, telemetry, anomaly detection, predictive modelling.
  • Also interested in AI applied to scientific computing and physics-aware modelling more broadly.

Theses

  • M.Sc. Thesis: Lower Bounds on the Mix Norm of Passive Scalars Advected by Incompressible Enstrophy-Constrained Flows (University of L'Aquila, 2021). Derived analytical lower bounds on the H⁻¹ mix norm using variational and functional-analytic methods, and simulated the Lin–Thiffeault–Doering optimal mixing velocity on the 2-torus with a pseudo-spectral solver. Thesis and code can be found here.
  • B.Sc. Thesis: Application of the Galerkin Finite Element Method to the One-Dimensional Stefan Problem (Obafemi Awolowo University, 2018).
