Subsequent Python implementation & numerical report

Updated in 2026 · Extension of the 2021 thesis topic

The linked numerical report documents the later computational work; it is distinct from the original submitted thesis.

A self-contained, open-source Python implementation of the Lin-Thiffeault-Doering (LTD) optimal-mixing scheme for passive scalar transport on the two-dimensional torus, ported from Gautam Iyer's original MATLAB code. Derives the LTD optimal velocity from first principles via a constrained-optimisation argument, documents every numerical design choice in the pseudo-spectral solver, is deterministically reproducible (a clean rerun matches its own previously recorded results to four decimal places in every entry), and adds a resolution-sensitivity study showing that the fitted mixing-rate exponent is itself resolution-dependent.

B.Sc. Thesis: Application of the Galerkin Finite Element Method to the One-Dimensional Stefan Problem

Obafemi Awolowo University, Nigeria · 2018

Applied Galerkin finite-element methods to a one-dimensional Stefan problem, formulating the weak problem and developing computational algorithms for tracking the moving phase boundary of this physically motivated free-boundary PDE.

Open-Source Research Software

Research implementations with public code, each addressing a specific mathematical or computational research question.

  • navier-stokes-2d: verified Fourier pseudo-spectral 2D incompressible Navier–Stokes solver with a POD-Galerkin reduced-order model and DEIM hyper-reduction.
  • fem-cylinder-flow: Taylor–Hood FEniCSx/PETSc finite-element solver, verified by manufactured solutions and benchmarked against the DFG Schäfer–Turek cylinder-wake test cases.
  • darcy-inverse-problem: deterministic adjoint-based and Bayesian (pCN/MCMC) inversion of a spatially varying Darcy permeability field.
  • fem-topology-optimization: verified 2D elasticity FEM driving a SIMP compliance-minimisation topology optimiser.
  • lorenz96-data-assimilation: stochastic Ensemble Kalman Filter with Gaspari–Cohn localisation for the chaotic Lorenz-96 system, independently validated against a linear Kalman filter.
  • boiling-phasefield-3d: phase-field solver coupling Allen-Cahn, Navier-Stokes, and an energy equation for multiphase-flow PDEs; a dedicated 1D Stefan solver converges at first order against the analytical benchmark, and the 3D implementation is numerically verified but not yet physically validated.
  • neural-surrogate-burgers: POD-Galerkin ROM, DEIM hyper-reduction, an MLP surrogate, and a Fourier Neural Operator compared on viscous Burgers' equation.
  • photoacoustic-reconstruction: sparse-view photoacoustic tomography, comparing time-reversal against a learned U-Net refinement.
  • pinn-advection-diffusion: physics-informed neural network for the 1D advection-diffusion equation, trained via automatic differentiation with no labelled data.
  • diff-pbr: differentiable physically-based renderer recovering material maps from multi-view images by gradient descent through the rendering equation.
  • nerf-tensorf: from-scratch implementations of NeRF and TensoRF, compared quantitatively under matched training conditions.
  • abdominal-ct-segmentation: 3D U-Net for liver segmentation from abdominal CT (Medical Segmentation Decathlon Task03), with a leakage-controlled full-volume evaluation pipeline implemented; independent evaluation is pending.
  • sss-dipole: differentiable implementation of the Donner & Jensen (2005) dipole BSSRDF for subsurface light transport.
