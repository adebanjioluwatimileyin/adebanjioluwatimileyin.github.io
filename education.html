Education

My mathematical training runs from foundational analysis through to the numerical methods and PDE theory that now underpin my research: each degree's thesis applied that training to a concrete computational problem rather than stopping at coursework.

  • Coursework: PDEs, Functional Analysis, Numerical Analysis, Numerical Methods for PDEs, Galerkin / Finite Element Methods, Scientific Computing, Dynamical Systems and Bifurcation Theory, Advanced Optimisation, Machine Learning, Fluid Dynamics
  • University of L'Aquila International Scholarship (2018 to 2021)

B.Sc. Mathematics

2014 to 2018 · Obafemi Awolowo University, Nigeria

  • MTN Foundation Scholarship  ·  PTDF Scholarship  ·  Murli T. Chellaram Foundation Scholarship (2014 to 2018)
