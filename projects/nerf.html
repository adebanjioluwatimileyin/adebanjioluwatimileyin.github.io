NeRF & TensoRF from scratch

Neural rendering

My contribution

I implemented NeRF and TensoRF and compared reconstruction quality on a held-out set of 40 images.

TensoRF: 16.7 dB test PSNR versus 13.7 dB for NeRF, using half the training iterations.

View source code ↗All projects

Problem

Reconstructing a continuous 3D scene representation from posed 2D images, and understanding the trade-off between an implicit MLP representation and an explicit tensor-decomposed one.

Approach

Vanilla NeRF and TensoRF (vector-matrix decomposition, Chen et al. 2022) are implemented from scratch in PyTorch, including an MPS-safe gather-based interpolation scheme for Apple Silicon, and compared under matched conditions.

Result

TensoRF reaches a higher PSNR than vanilla NeRF in half the training iterations (15k vs 30k), measured on a held-out test split of 40 images.

+3 dBvs Vanilla NeRF (13.7 dB)
