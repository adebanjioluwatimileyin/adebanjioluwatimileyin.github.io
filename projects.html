Research

Phase-Field Modelling of Multiphase-Flow PDEs

Research · Scientific Computing · PDEs · Current work

Problem: modelling the coupled interface, flow, and heat-transfer physics of boiling (vapour-liquid phase change under turbulent flow) with a PDE formulation that stays numerically tractable and portable to 3D. Method: implemented a conservative Allen-Cahn phase-field model coupled to incompressible Navier-Stokes and an energy equation with a latent-heat source term (following Roccon, 2025), using an FFT-based direct solver for the resulting constant-coefficient pressure Poisson equation, a property that holds in 3D and is the main motivation for the method. Result: the 2D/1D prototype reproduces the analytical bubble-growth and Stefan-problem solutions; the 3D extension is implemented and is the current focus of ongoing validation work. Code →

Optimal Mixing of Passive Scalars

Research · Scientific ML · PDEs · M.Sc. thesis

Problem: how efficiently can an incompressible, enstrophy-constrained velocity field mix a passive scalar, and what are the theoretical limits on that mixing rate? Method: derived analytical lower bounds on the H⁻¹ mix norm using variational and functional-analytic methods, then simulated the Lin-Thiffeault-Doering optimal mixing velocity on the 2-torus with a pseudo-spectral solver, Leray projection, and RK45 time integration. Result: confirmed exponential H⁻¹ decay across initial conditions, with mixing timescale scaling as predicted by the theoretical lower bound as the support parameter a varies. Code →

Physics-Informed Neural Network: 1D Advection-Diffusion

Research · Scientific Machine Learning

Problem: approximating the solution of a linear PDE (1D advection-diffusion) with a neural network trained without any labelled interior data. Method: a PyTorch PINN enforcing the PDE residual, initial condition, and periodic boundary condition simultaneously via automatic differentiation, trained on collocation points with Adam. Result: relative L² error of ~5×10⁻³ against the known analytical solution across the full space-time domain. Code →

3D Liver Segmentation: Abdominal CT

Research · Medical Imaging

Problem: automatic segmentation of liver structures from abdominal CT volumes for downstream clinical and computational analysis. Method: 3D U-Net trained end-to-end on 131 MSD Task03 liver CT cases, with foreground-biased patch sampling, a combined soft-Dice + BCE loss, cosine learning-rate annealing, and Gaussian sliding-window inference. Result: best validation Dice 0.9886, HD95 ≤ 1 mm, after 200 epochs on a single Kaggle T4 GPU. Code →

Differentiable PBR: Inverse Material Optimisation

Research · Inverse Rendering

Problem: recovering spatially-varying surface material properties (albedo, normal, roughness, metallic) from only a set of multi-view photographs. Method: pure-PyTorch differentiable renderer implementing the Cook-Torrance BRDF (GGX normal distribution, Schlick Fresnel, Smith geometry term), optimizing material maps directly via gradient descent against 8 reference views, with no external differentiable-rasterization library. Result: converged material maps after 2,000 optimization steps, reaching an MSE of ~1×10⁻⁶ against target renders. Code →

NeRF vs TensoRF: Neural Radiance Fields

Research · Neural Rendering

Problem: reconstructing a continuous 3D scene representation from posed 2D images, and understanding the trade-off between an implicit MLP representation and an explicit tensor-decomposed one. Method: implemented vanilla NeRF and TensoRF (vector-matrix decomposition, Chen et al. 2022) from scratch in PyTorch, including an MPS-safe gather-based interpolation scheme for Apple Silicon, and compared them under matched conditions. Result: TensoRF reached 17.2 dB PSNR (+3 dB over vanilla NeRF's 14.2 dB) in half the training iterations (15k vs 30k). Code →

Applied ML / Engineering

Aircraft Engine Monitoring via Audio DSP

Applied ML / Engineering · Signal Processing

Problem: estimating aircraft engine RPM and operational status in real time without dedicated tachometer hardware. Method: FFT-based signal processing on microphone audio running on a Raspberry Pi, transmitting extracted RPM and status over UART to an ESP32 controller for real-time display. Result: validated against a real Tecnam P92 / Rotax 912 engine, demonstrating accurate RPM extraction from acoustic signal alone. Code →
