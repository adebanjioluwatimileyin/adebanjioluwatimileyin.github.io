Research Outputs

Thesis

M.Sc. Thesis: Lower Bounds on the Mix Norm of Passive Scalars Advected by Incompressible Enstrophy-Constrained Flows

University of L'Aquila, Italy · 2021

Derived analytical lower bounds on the H⁻¹ mix norm of a passive scalar under an enstrophy-constrained incompressible velocity field, using variational and functional-analytic methods, and validated the predicted mixing behaviour with a pseudo-spectral numerical solver (the Lin-Thiffeault-Doering optimal mixing velocity on the 2-torus).

B.Sc. Thesis: Application of the Galerkin Finite Element Method to the One-Dimensional Stefan Problem

Obafemi Awolowo University, Nigeria · 2018

Applied Galerkin finite-element methods to a one-dimensional Stefan problem, formulating the weak problem and developing computational algorithms for tracking the moving phase boundary of this physically motivated free-boundary PDE.

Open-Source Research Software

Research implementations with public code, each addressing a specific mathematical or computational research question.

  • boiling-phasefield-3d: phase-field solver coupling Allen-Cahn, Navier-Stokes, and an energy equation for multiphase-flow PDEs; current work extending a validated 2D/1D prototype toward 3D.
  • pinn-advection-diffusion: physics-informed neural network for the 1D advection-diffusion equation, trained via automatic differentiation with no labelled data.
  • diff-pbr: differentiable physically-based renderer recovering material maps from multi-view images by gradient descent through the rendering equation.
  • nerf-tensorf: from-scratch implementations of NeRF and TensoRF, compared quantitatively under matched training conditions.
  • abdominal-ct-segmentation: 3D U-Net for liver segmentation from abdominal CT, with reproducible training and evaluation on the Medical Segmentation Decathlon.
  • sss-dipole: differentiable implementation of the Donner & Jensen (2005) dipole BSSRDF for subsurface light transport.
